 6 · CHUNKED vs CHUNKLESS — THE COST GAP (per question)

   per 14-question run, per question:
      fixed_char  1 call(s), ~297 in-tokens → $0.000404
      structural  1 call(s), ~824 in-tokens → $0.000683
      agentic     2 call(s), ~2125 in-tokens → $0.001716
      (agentic's ctx in the grid counts only the units it read in full; its bill above
      adds the full-manual navigation scan — that scan is what makes it 2 calls.)

      agentic sends ~7.1× the context of fixed_char and ~2.6× of structural,
      with 2× the calls — and ~2.5× the price of structural,
      the method that actually wins on completeness. Structural's top-2 is bigger than
      fixed_char's (whole sections vs tiny slices), yet it stays far below agentic and
      needs ONE call: equal-k is an honest ruler only because the token column shows the
      real budget each method spends. On a 5,000-word regulation the agentic context
      (whole sections + a full-document navigation scan) multiplies while structural's
      stays section-sized — that is why real systems chunk.

 7 · HONEST LIMITS OF THIS DEMO
