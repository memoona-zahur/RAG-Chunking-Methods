 6 · CHUNKED vs CHUNKLESS — THE COST GAP
      chunked (fixed-size): 1 LLM call/question, only the small top-2 chunk as context.
      agentic (no chunks)  : 2 calls/question (navigate + answer), whole sections read.

   method       | LLM calls | context tokens | est. USD (4 questions)
   ----------------------------------------------------------
   chunked      |         8 |           3291 |          0.00213
   agentic      |         8 |           3530 |          0.00236

      agentic reads ~0.9x the context tokens of chunked for the same 4 questions
      and burns 2 calls instead of 1. On a small manual the gap is modest,
      but chunked context is PREDICTABLE (top-k x chunk size) while agentic
      context grows with whole-section size - flip it to a 5,000-word section
      and the gap multiplies. Structure is the cheap way to keep completeness
      and small fixed reads: structural got 4/4 questions complete in one call each.
