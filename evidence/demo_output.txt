 6 · CHUNKED vs CHUNKLESS — THE COST GAP (per question)
      EVERY figure below is a REAL LLM call: both sides actually answer on Groq.
      chunked shown for fixed_char : 1 call/question, only the small top-2 chunks as context.
      agentic (no chunks)          : 2 calls/question (real navigation + answer), whole sections read.

   method              | calls | tokens in | tokens out | est. USD (4 questions)
   ----------------------------------------------------------------------
   chunked (fixed_char) |     4 |     1184 |      1182 |              0.00163
   agentic (chunkless)  |     8 |     8524 |      2354 |              0.00689

   per 4-question run, per question:
      chunked: 1 call, ~296 in-tokens → $0.000408
      agentic: 2 calls, ~2131 in-tokens → $0.001722

      agentic bills ~7.2× the context tokens, 2× the calls,
      and ~4.2× the price per question — even before whole sections get big.
      On a small manual the gap is modest, but chunked context is PREDICTABLE
      (top-k × chunk size) while agentic context grows with whole-section size —
      flip it to a 5,000-word regulation and the gap multiplies. Structure is the
      cheap way to keep completeness and small fixed reads: structural got 4/4
      questions complete in one call each.

 7 · HONEST LIMITS OF THIS DEMO
  - one document, four questions, one embedding model: a micro-benchmark, not a law.
  - a real production decision needs your own documents + question set + reranking.
  - $ figures use example pricing; check the Groq console (openai/gpt-oss-120b) before quoting.
